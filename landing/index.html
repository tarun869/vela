The loop

Observe. Reason. Act. Repeat every 15 minutes.

Six stages. One continuous cycle. Every iteration compounds the advantage.

01
Observe
Ingest asset telemetry, market prices, weather forecasts, and ERCOT grid signals in real time. Every data point feeds the next decision.
02
Forecast
Predict LMP spikes, load curves, and opt-out risk across a 4-hour horizon using ERCOT wind ramp signals and ORDC overlays.
03
Reason
Weigh energy arbitrage, demand response, ancillary services, degradation cost, backup reserve, and customer constraints simultaneously.
04
Act
Generate bids, dispatch assets, auto-approve low-risk decisions, and route high-risk ones to the human review queue with full rationale attached.
05
Measure
Verify delivery against commitments to the 15-minute interval. Attribute revenue per asset. Flag underperformance with root-cause analysis.
06
Learn
Update strategy models based on settlement outcomes. Every cycle makes the next one sharper. The advantage compounds automatically.
